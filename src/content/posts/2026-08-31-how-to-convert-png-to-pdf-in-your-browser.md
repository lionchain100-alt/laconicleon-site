When several PNG images need to become one document, the main challenge is often keeping the pages in the correct order. A browser-based PNG-to-PDF tool can be a practical option when you want a quick result without installing software or creating another account.

[PNG Binder](https://pngbinder.com/) converts up to 50 PNG images into one ordered PDF. You can arrange the images, choose the page size, and download the completed document directly from your browser.

## How to convert PNG to PDF

1. Open [PNG Binder](https://pngbinder.com/).
2. Add the PNG images you want to include.
3. Drag the images into the correct page order.
4. Choose automatic, A4, or US Letter page sizing.
5. Create and download the finished PDF.

Before creating the PDF, check the image order carefully. The first image becomes the first page, the second image becomes the second page, and so on.

## Choose the right page size

Automatic sizing follows the dimensions of each image. This is useful when preserving the original proportions matters.

A4 and US Letter are better choices when the PDF will be printed or shared as a conventional document. You can also adjust orientation, margins, and image fitting before creating the file.

## Browser-local processing

PNG Binder processes the conversion in your browser. Your images are not uploaded to a conversion server, and the tool does not require an account.

There is also no watermark, payment, advertising, Analytics, or OCR. This keeps the workflow focused on one task: turning images into an ordered PDF.

## Other supported formats

PNG is the primary format, but JPG and JPEG images are also supported. You can use up to 50 supported images in one conversion.

If you need a simple way to convert PNG to PDF, try [PNG Binder](https://pngbinder.com/) free in your browser.
