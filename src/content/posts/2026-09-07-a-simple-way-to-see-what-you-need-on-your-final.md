Finals bring a very specific question: what score do I need on the final to reach the course grade I want?

GradeClarity is a free, browser-based final grade calculator built for that planning step. Enter your current grade and the percentage your final is worth, then see the score needed for different targets, the range of possible final course grades, and which targets are still mathematically reachable.

It is a planning tool, not an official grade. Course policies, curves, points systems, and passing cutoffs differ, so the syllabus and instructor remain the source of truth. GradeClarity lets you adjust the grading scale and passing example to match those rules.

The calculator runs in the browser. It does not collect, store, or transmit the grades entered into it. There is no sign-up.

Try the calculator here: https://gradeclarity.com/
