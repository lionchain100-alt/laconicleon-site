Preparing a classroom review activity often starts with a simple list of words. The hard part is turning that list into cards that are easy to print and hand out. A free bingo card generator can handle the repetitive layout work while you focus on the lesson. Start with one term on each line and remove duplicates. For a classroom vocabulary activity, 24 words leave one free center square on a standard 5x5 card. Choose the number of cards, paper size, and cards per page. In this example, use 30 cards on A4 paper with two cards per page. Preview the title, word fit, and card count before printing, then download the PDF locally and check the pages once more. MakeBingoCards is a free browser-based bingo card generator for teachers, tutors, ESL lessons, and small-group hosts: https://makebingocards.com/ No sign-up or payment is required for the free workflow.
